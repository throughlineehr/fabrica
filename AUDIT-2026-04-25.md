### Fixed today

1. **RoomShell back button label-in-name mismatch (2.5.3).**
   `RoomShell.jsx:60` — visible text was `tr('nav.esc')` ("esc"),
   `aria-label` was `tr('nav.back')` ("Back"). Visible label not
   in accessible name. Fixed: visible text now `← {tr('nav.back')}`.
2. **SignalFeed time format not localized (3.1.2).**
   `SignalFeed.jsx:7` — `toLocaleTimeString([], …)` used browser
   locale, not app locale. Fixed: now reads `lang` from
   `useTranslation()` and passes it through.

### v0.2.x regressions

- **`WiringDemo`** has its own `role="status" aria-live="polite"`
  live region and full keyboard parity with mouse — patching,
  detaching, deleting all reachable. Single hardcoded English
  strings throughout, but it's a styleguide demo (acceptable; flagged
  in DEBT for the day it gets promoted).
- **`Digest` processor** — emits standard `narrative` and `alert`
  signals into the SignalFeed which already has `role="log"
  aria-live="polite"`. No new a11y surface.

### Open i18n work (3.1.2)

Locale parity is the largest remaining WCAG item. `~50` keys
per non-English locale fall back to English (the keys exist in
`en.js`; the values in other locale files are stubs or English
duplicates). Specifically:

- `systemPage.*` cluster (audit, incoming, outgoing, processors,
  addProcessor, processorLibrary, noProcessors, inputs, outputs,
  filterTypes, filterTags, filterTerminals, etc.) — missing real
  translations in ar, es, fr, hi, id, it, ja, ru, zh.
- `settings.colorBlindMode` — translated only in Russian; all other
  locales have the English string under that key.
- Russian: `agent.intro` partially in English; several `agent.*`
  keys (`cannotAddOperation`, `cannotRemoveRoot`, `wentBack`) appear
  truncated.

Translation is mechanical but needs speakers, not heuristics. Listed
in DEBT.md.

**Grade: A-.** All structural a11y is in place. The remaining work
is i18n backfill.
